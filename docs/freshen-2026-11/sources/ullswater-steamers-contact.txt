How to get to Ullswater ‘Steamers’ by car
Travelling from the North
Leave the M6 at Junction 40 and follow signs towards Pooley Bridge (approximately 10 minutes) or Glenridding (around 20–30 minutes).
Travelling from the South
Leave the M6 at Junction 36 and follow the route via Windermere and over Kirkstone Pass. Glenridding is approximately 55 minutes from the motorway.
Parking at Glenridding
We have a pay and display car park on-site at Glenridding. The machine for this is just at the bottom of the ramp as you approach the pier houses. It takes cash or contactless cards.
There is also a large pay and display car park in the village, just a short walk from our pier house
We have 1 x  double 11kW EV charger on site located by the picnic area. Charges apply. Please download the Monta app in advance of your visit and follow the instructions to charge your vehicle. Please note that Ullswater Steamers do not manage the app services. Please contact Monta directly via the app if you have any queries or issues. Please note that car parking charges apply whilst using the EV.
Download Monta App
Parking at Pooley Bridge
There are two pay and display car parks on either side of the bridge and extra car parking in a field signposted in the village.
More information can be found  here
Parking at Aira Force
There is a National Trust car park at Aira Force, just a short walk away from our pier.
The postcode for this car park is  CA11 0JS.
Parking at Howtown
Due to its secluded nature, there are no car parking facilities available at Howtown.

Get to Ullswater ‘Steamers’ by bus
Leave the car behind and take the bus to Ullswater ‘Steamers’ for a beautiful Lake District cruise. Our timetable has been carefully planned to align with the 508 Stagecoach bus service, which links Penrith and Pooley Bridge with Glenridding daily throughout the year. Plan your journey with Stagecoach’s winter timetable here.
Please note:
Check Stagecoach bus timetables are up to date before travelling.

Get to Ullswater ‘Steamers’ by train
For a more sustainable day out why not travel by train to Ullswater ‘Steamers’ for your lake cruise?
Travel to Penrith on Avanti West Coast mainline or Transpennine Cross Country services. Connect with a Stagecoach bus from Penrith train station to Ullswater ‘Steamers’ piers at Pooley Bridge or Glenridding. Save money with a combined Bus and Boat ticket and enjoy a car free day out.
Please note:
Ullswater ‘Steamers’ nearest train station is Penrith on the West Coast mainline. Direct services to/from Glasgow, Carlisle, Manchester, London.
Check train and bus timetables connections align with your chosen Ullswater ‘Steamers’ cruise times and departure point.
