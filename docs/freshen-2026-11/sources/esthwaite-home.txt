Discover Esthwaite Water
From world-class trout and pike fishing to peaceful boat hire and unforgettable osprey encounters, Esthwaite Water offers some of the Lake District's most unique outdoor experiences. Whether you're visiting for a few hours or planning a longer stay, explore everything the lake has to offer below.
COARSE/BANK FISHING
Esthwaite Water is renowned for its specimen perch fishing and thriving coarse fish stocks. Target perch, roach and bream from the bank or boat in one of the Lake District's most unique coarse fishing venues.
View More
OSPREY SAFARI
Experience the resident ospreys of Esthwaite Water. Guided and self-guided trips offer an unforgettable wildlife experience in the heart of the Lake District.
View More
TROUT FISHING
Fish one of the UK's most renowned trout fisheries. Esthwaite Water offers exceptional fly fishing, boat fishing and tuition in the heart of the Lake District.
View More
PIKE FISHING
Target specimen pike at one of the Lake District's most renowned predator fishing venues. Esthwaite Water offers excellent pike fishing from both bank and boat.
View More
BOAT HIRE
Explore Esthwaite Water at your own pace with self-drive boat hire. Perfect for families, couples and wildlife lovers looking to experience one of the Lake District's most beautiful lakes from the water.
View More
COMPETITIONS & EVENTS
Take part in exciting fishing competitions throughout the season, including perch, pike and specialist angling events at one of the Lake District's most iconic fisheries.
View More

Hill Top
Visit Beatrix Potter’s Home
Discover the home of Beatrix Potter, one of the Lake District’s most beloved figures. Located just minutes from Esthwaite Water, Hill Top offers visitors the chance to step inside the house and gardens that inspired many of her famous stories.
The surrounding countryside and quiet lanes around Esthwaite still reflect the landscapes that shaped her work, making this the perfect stop before or after visiting The Boathouse.  More Info
Discover Grasmere & Dove Cottage
Explore The Literary Heart Of The Lakes
Visit the charming village of Grasmere and discover Dove Cottage, once home to poet William Wordsworth. Surrounded by dramatic fells and peaceful walking routes, this area captures the timeless beauty that has inspired generations of writers and artists.
Visitors can explore independent cafés, traditional Lake District shops and scenic walking routes before returning to the quieter surroundings of Esthwaite Water.

Hiking & Walking Around Esthwaite Water
Explore The Lake District On Foot
Esthwaite Water sits at the centre of some of the Lake District’s most scenic walking routes. From gentle lakeside strolls to full-day fell walks, the area offers routes for every level of adventure.
Nearby walks include woodland trails, waterside paths and routes connecting Hawkshead, Grizedale Forest and surrounding viewpoints across the Lakes.
Visitors often combine morning walks with coffee at The Boathouse or end the day with pizza beside the water.
