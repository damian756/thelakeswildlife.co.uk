About Us
Kent Westmorland Angling Association is one of the oldest fishing clubs in the UK. Founded in 1848, we control and manage 8 miles of game fishing on the beautiful River Kent and coarse fishing on Killington Reservoir.
Our club manages and promotes conservation and fish care, recommending catch and release, barbless hook fishing for salmon and sea trout.
