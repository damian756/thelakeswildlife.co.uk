Ridding Wood trail - easy woodland walk
Starting from the Visitor Centre car park, this trail is accessible and offers a great introduction to the forest and the sculptures. It passes through beautiful oak woodland as well as some unusual and ornate trees. Will you find the Gruffalo hiding in the Deep Dark Wood?
You can now access this route by off-road mobility scooter (tramper) through Outdoor Mobility - learn more HERE .
Trail map
Trail information
Grizedale, Grizedale Forest, Hawkshead, LA22 0QJ
Opening hours
Get directions
Tarmac surface. Follow the brown markers.
Grade - Easy Access
Trail Length
1 mile (1.5 km)
