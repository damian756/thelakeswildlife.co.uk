Forestry England, "Walking trails at Grizedale" map leaflet, ref "2310 / Mar 26". PDF fetched 11 Nov 2026 (linked from https://www.forestryengland.uk/grizedale). Text transcribed from the PDF.

Named walking trails (complete list on the leaflet):
- Ridding Wood Trail, 1.5km (1 mile), 45 minutes. "The shortest and easiest trail in Grizedale"
- Grizedale Tarn Trail, 7km (4.5 miles), 2 hours
- Silurian Way, 18km (11 miles), 6 hours
- Millwood Trail, short 1km / long 2.5km
- Carron Crag Trail, 8km (5 miles), 3 hours, "the highest point in the forest" (Carron Crag 314m)
- Bogle Crag Trail, 5km (3 miles), 2 hours, starts at Bogle Crag car park
No "Courthouse Trail" appears on the leaflet or map. The leaflet does not mention red squirrels or squirrel feeders.
Grizedale Centre grid ref SD 336 944; Moor Top car park SD 343 965.
