Buy a rod fishing licence for England and Wales
You can buy a 1-day, 8-day or 12-month licence online for yourself or someone else.
You can buy a licence up to 30 days before you want it to start.
This guide is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .
Fees
There are no discounted prices for a 1-day or an 8-day licence.

Your licence fee will be used to fund work such as:
stocking fish

You need different permissions or licences to fish in most of Scotland and Northern Ireland .
Eligibility for disabled licences
You can get a 12-month disabled licence if one of the following applies:
you have a Blue Badge or meet the eligibility criteria for one
you get Personal Independence Payment (PIP) - any rate
you get Disability Living Allowance (DLA)
If you get PIP or DLA, you’ll need to give your National Insurance number (or child reference number if you’re under 16) when you apply.
Get a licence online
You can get an adult or junior licence online. Junior licences for children aged 13 to 16 are free.
You’ll need:
a debit or credit card
your National Insurance number, if you get PIP or DLA and you’re applying for a 12-month licence
the other person’s details (such as date of birth), if you’re buying for someone else

Other ways to get a licence
You can also get an adult or junior licence by calling the Environment Agency.

If you have not received your licence
Licences sent by post should arrive within 10 working days. Licences sent by email or text message usually arrive within one hour.
Contact the Environment Agency if you have not received your licence.
