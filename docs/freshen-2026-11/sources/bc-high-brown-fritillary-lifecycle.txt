High Brown Fritillary lifecycle chart (an image on https://butterfly-conservation.org/butterflies/high-brown-fritillary, "Lifecycle" section; the page has no flight period in its text).
Fetched 11 Nov 2026. Read from the chart (one row per stage, January to December):

Egg: all year except roughly May to mid-August (the overwintering stage).
Caterpillar: March to early June, strongest in April.
Chrysalis: mid-May to early July, strongest in June.
Adult: from mid-June to mid-August, strongest in July. One adult period a year.

Used for the site's flight period: "June to August, mostly in July".
