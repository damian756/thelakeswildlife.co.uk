About the #FoulshawOspreys
We've had ospreys nesting at our Foulshaw Moss Nature Reserve since at least 2014, when we first discovered our now-established pair, male White YW and female Blue 35.
In 2025, a second osprey pair made a home at the site on a human-made nesting platform – male Blue 476 and female Blue 717. We're excited to share both pairs' stories going forward.
The ospreys arrive at the site around the end of March each year, and usually migrate south by the start of September.
