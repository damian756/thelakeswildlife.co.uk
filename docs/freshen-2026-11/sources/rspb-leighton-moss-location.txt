Getting here
Car Car Bus Bus Train Train Bike Bike
Leighton Moss is near the village of Silverdale and just 3km (2 miles) from Carnforth. Follow the brown tourist signs from J35 of the M6.
there is a bus stop right outside the visitor centre, next to the main car park. The numbers 49, 51 and 550 stop at Leighton Moss.
For details of timetables please check the 'Getting here' section on our 'About' page.
Silverdale station is on the Manchester Airport/Preston to Barrow line and is only 250 metres from the reserve.
Turn left out of the station entrance, take care walking along the road, then left again and then right into the reserve entrance.
Moss is just off National Cycle Route 6 and Regional route 90 (Lancashire Cycleway). Bike racks are available in the accessible car park in front of the visitor centre. The Causeway is a public bridleway and can be accessed on bike. Please do not cycle on the other footpaths around the reserve.
